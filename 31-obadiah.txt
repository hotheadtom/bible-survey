Obadiah - Servant/Worshipper of God
Prophecy about Edom (v1)
Shortest book of Old Testament

Main Topic - your heart has deceived you (3)
Main Message - As you have done, it will be done to you;
                your deeds will return upon your own head (15)

1-16 Total destruction of Edom because they rejoice at the misery of Israel
17-21 Restoration of Israel. 

There are 13 Obadiah's in the Bible
No personal information about this Obadiah

Lived in Judah.
Timeline unknown.

-----
1-14 - Accusations against Edom
        Edom's pride and self exaltation
        Pludered Israel cities and abused refugees when Babylon invaded Israel (Ezek 35)

15 - The day of the Lord is near against all nations

16-21 The day of the Lord for all nations
        Restoration of God's kingdom over Jerusalem

Edom ~ Adam (humanity)

Edom's pride is an example of human condition
Edom's downfall points to the coming of God's kingdom over all nations
