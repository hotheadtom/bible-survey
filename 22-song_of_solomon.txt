Song of Songs/Song of Solomon/Canticle of Canticles

8 Chapters -  Author Solomon

Topic - Love
Message - Union between Christ and the Church

1:2-2:7 Initial Love
2:8-3:5 Faltering love
3:6-5:1 Growing love
5:2-7:14 Transforming love
8:1-14 Mature love

contains 5-13 songs

Beloved (He) and Love (She Shulemite woman)

Charcters
    Shulemite woman
    Her family - Mother, 2 sisters, 2 brothers, father passed away
    Daughters of Israel
    Bridegroom

Setting of the drama: Jerusalem palace

Shulemite woman - from a farming family Ephraim hills
    dark and beautiful. suntanned working in Solomon's vineyard as the eldest daughter.
    worked as shepherdess at roadside on a hillslope

Bridegroom - is not a normal person - takes her to a banqueting house. He doesn't have sheep

Bridegroom leaves the woman and she waits for him.

she realizes that her bridegroom is king Solomon

---------------------

1:2-7 Introduction

8:6-14 Conclusion
    The power and intensity of love - beautiful & lifegiving  or  dangerous & destructive

Main voice is of a woman
Man's voice isn't Solomon
Solomon mentioned, but never speaks

Both man and woman are engaged and want to be together
Theme One: Intense desire: Seeking and finding
    But they get separated. They  keep looking for each other
Theme Two: The joy of physical attraction

at the end Solomon tries to buy love, but the woman rejects.

Interpretations
1) Jewish - An allegory of Israel & God. Love is a symbol of the covenant made at Mt. Sinai
2) Christian - An allegory of Chuch & Christ.
3) Collection of ancient poetry that reflects on the divine gift of love

Garden imagery echos Garden of Eden
