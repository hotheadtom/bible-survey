Jeramiah lived in the later half on 7th century and in the first half of 6th century.
Lived during the rule of Judah kings - Josiah, Jehoahaz, Jehoiakim, Jehoiachin, Zedekiah.

Warned the people of exile due to idolatry and social injustice 
Tells the people will come back from exile after 70 years (25:11)

No topical or chronological order is followed by Jeremiah

Weeping prophet

many biographical contents

52 chapters

1 The call 
2-38 The message to Israel and Judah before the fall of Judah 
39 Fall of Judah
40-44  Message to the remnant
45-50 Message to various nations - Philistines, Moab, Ammonites, Edom, Damascus
51-52 Flashback

son of Hilkiah the priest
prohibited from marrying
he was persecuted, imprisoned 
he was forced to go to Egypt, where he continues his ministry 
40 years of ministry

not mighty as Elijah, or eloquent as Isiah or did not have the great visions as Ezekiel
