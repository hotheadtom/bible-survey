3 kinds of parallelisms
    synonyms  - 2nd line of the proverb repeats the same idea as the first
                eg Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep,
                    and an idle person will suffer hunger.(19:15)
    contrast - 2nd line has opposite idea to explain the meaning of the first line
                eg The light of the righteous rejoices,
                    but the lamp of the wicked will be put out. (13:9)
    synthetic - 2nd line completes the idea of the first
                eg The terror of a king is like the growling of a lion;
                    whoever provokes him to anger forfeits his life. (20:2)
