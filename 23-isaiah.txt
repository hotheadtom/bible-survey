Isaiah lived in the latter half of Israel's kingdom period

Message
    Judgement: Israel's rebellion will come at a cost. Assyria & Babylon will come
    Hope: Fulfillment of covenant promises. Future king & God's blessings to the nations

1-12: Judgement & Hope for Jerusalem
    1-2: Old Jerusalem, Rebellion, Idolatory & Injustice -> Purifying Fire -> New Jerusalem (Justice & Peace for all nations)
    6: Isaiah's Temple vision, Isaiah's calling, difficult mission that will harden hearts as they have reached a point of no return. Burning stump will be Holy Seed.
    7-12: Announces downfall to Ahaz. God will raise a new king Immanuel. The Holy Seed.

13-27: Fall of Babylon & Israel's neighbors
    13-23
        Assyria will be replaced by Babylon
        Babylon will be brought down
        Philistia, Moab, Egypt, Tyre, Damascus all will be brought down.
    24-27: A tale of 2 cities
        The lofty rebellious city that is destined for ruin
        The new Jeruasalem - God's kingdom over all nations, no more suffering or death
        
28-39: The rise and fall of Jerusalem
    28-35: Accusation of Jerusalem's leaders for turning to Egypt for military protection against Assyria. Turn to God & repent.
    36-38: Hezekiah's rise. Hezekiah turns to God and is miraculously delivered from Assyrians.
    39: Hezekiah's fall. Hosts a delegation from Babylon. Isaiah predicts this ally will betray and destroy Jerusalem. 
        Prophesy fullfilled after 100 years 2Kings 24-25

40-48: Announcement of hope & comfort
    40: Exile is over (42: Israel should become God's servant to the nations). Israel accuses God(40:27)
    41-47: The trial
        God's response:
            Exile was judgement, not neglect.
            For Israel's sake God raised Persia to conquer Babylon
        Conclusion: Israel's God is the true God
    48: Israel is still as hard hearted as their ancestors.
        God is going to do a new thing.
 
49-55: The Servant fulfills God's mission
   49: God's servant. You are now Israel
        Mission:
            To restore Israel
            To be a light to the nations
       Sounds like the Messianic king
       But is rejected and killed. Servan't death is a sacrifice for sins
       But lives again (53:10-12)
   54-55: 2 ways to respond
        Are humble and repent - the servants
        Reject the servant - the wicked

56-66: The servants inherit God's kingdom
    56a: All nations invited to join God's covenant family
    56b-58: Contrast between the wicked and the servants
    59: Prayer of repentance
    60-62: Servant announces God's kingdom 
    65-66a: Contrast between the wicked and the servants
    65-66a : Prayer of repentance
    66b: All nations invited to join God's covenant family
