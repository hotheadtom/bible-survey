Book of captivities

25 chapters. Author unknown

10 northern tribes -> Assyria. Did not return (17:22-23)
2 sourthern tribes -> Babylon. Temple, palace, houses burnt. Wall destroyed. Remnant returned. (25:21)

1-10 Israel's kingdoms. Elisha's ministry
11-17 Both kingdoms. Prophets - Jonah, Amos, Hosea
18-25 Judah's kingdom & exile. 8 prophets - Obadiah, Joel, Isaiah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Jeremiah

Israel - 19 kings (8 in 1st kings & 11 in 2nd kings)
Judah - 20 kings (4 in 1st kings & 16 in 2nd kings)

Main Topic - According to the word of God
Main Message - Fulfillment of the word of God to the saints and the sinners sure and certain

Where there is no prophetic vision the people cast off restraint(Prov 29:18)

66 years of Elisha's ministry. 16 miracles.

Hezekiah - one of the best kings. son Manasseh worst king.

Josiah - Before him there was no king like him, who turned to the Lord with all his heart and with all his soul and with all his might, according to all the Law of Moses, nor did any like him arise after him(23:25)

Joash -  But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being put to death, and she put him and his nurse in a bedroom(11:2)

 And Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the secretary, "I have found the Book of the Law in the house of the Lord." (22:8)

 Repeating phrases - 
    Man of God
    Anger of the Lord
    he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord
    he did what was evil in the sight of the Lord
