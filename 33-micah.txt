Micah was from Moresheth in Judah
Was a contemporary of Isaiah, Amos, Hosea


He has told you, O man, what is good;
    and what does the Lord require of you
but to do justice, and to love kindness,
    and to walk humbly with your God? (6:8)

He will again have compassion on us;
    he will tread our iniquities underfoot.
You will cast all our sins
    into the depths of the sea. (7:19)

Main message: Present judgement & future blessing

1-3 Proclamation of judgement due to greed, idolatory, violence, immorality. Will be done by Assyrians.
	Fall of Samaria (BC 721) (1:6)
	Exile (BC 702) (1:16)
	Fall of Jerusalem (BC 586) (3:12, 7:13)
	Exile to Babylon (BC 586) (4:10)
	Return from captivity (4:1-8, 7:11)
	The birthplace of Jesus (5:2)

4-5 Promise of restoration
6-7 Call to repentence

Micah - Who is like Jehovah?

--------------------------------

Predicted Assyria would come and ravage northern kingdom and after that Babylon will come

1-2 Accusations and warnings
	God appears to bring his judgement
		Leaders - wealthy through theft and greed
		Prophets - money for favorable prophesy
	2:12-13
		God will shepherd the remnant of his people

3-4a Injustice of Israel's leaders
		Illegal sale of family land (Torah violation; Leviticus 25:23, Numbers 36:7)
		Disaster is coming

	4:1-7 New Jerusalem - Remnant restored. Peace on Earth

4b - 5 Assyrian - Babylonian exile - Restoration
	New Ruler from Bethlehem
	5:1-6 Messianic kingdom

	5:7-14 God's kingdom blesses the nations
		The remnant among the nations

6-7 Israel's unjust economic practices 
	7:8-20 Israel personified as a suffering defeated individual. Begs God's mercy.

	Reasons for future hope:
		God's character
		God's promises
