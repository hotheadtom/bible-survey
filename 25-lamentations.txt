Lament poems
    A form of protest
    A way to process emotion
    A place to voice confusion
    Restore a sacred dignity to human suffering

Chapters 1-4 are acrostics (alphabet poems). Each verse begins with a new letter of hebrew alphabet
Ordered structure vs disorder & pain & confusion

1 Lady Zion's grief and shame
    Jerusalem - daughter of Zion, widow 
2 The fall of Jerusalem & God's wrath
3 Three verses per letter
    Language drawn from Job, Psalms and Isaiah 53
    God's judgment is the seedbed of hope
4 Same structure as 1 and 2
  The siege of Jerusalem
  The past vs the siege - the children, rich, royals..
5 Breaks the alpahbet design pattern
  Poets grief explodes
  Communal prayer for God's mercy
  Lists all the different kinds of people devastated by the fall.
