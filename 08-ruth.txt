Occurred during the time of the judges
One of the 2 books named after a woman
Moabite woman who married an Israelite (as opposed to Esther who married a foreigner)
One of 4 women in Jesus' lineage (others are Tamar, Rahab and Bathsheba)
Ruth - virtous woman(3:11, 4:15)

4 chapters - Author assumed to be Samuel

1 - Love's choice (thiranjeduppu) decision
2 - Love's response (prathikaranam) service
3 - Love's security (surakshithathvam) rest
4 - Love's reward (prathibhalam) blessing

Theme - rest(1:9, 3:1, 3:18)/redemption(4:4,6)

Message - Power of pure love to overcome difficulties

Potrait of Christ - Boaz kinsman redeemer
