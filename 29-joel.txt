Believed to be written during the reign of Joash (one of the earlier prophets in Judea)
Joash might have seen Elijah/Elisha during childhood

describes the locust attack, drought in palestine.
God punishes those who opposes his just will
The day of the Lord is near.
Invites people to repentence

2:28 - 2:32 Promise of the Spirit

3:18 - 3:21 Restoration of Judea

Main Topic - Day of the Lord
Main Message - The importance & value of repentence
